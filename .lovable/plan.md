# Reframe Campaign 04 around distance, urgency, and inheritance

## Goal
Make **One Story, Together** feel like a real MemReel experience for families separated by distance. The story will create urgency around what a child can lose between visits, then show how MemReel brings a grandparent’s stories and voice into the child’s life.

The campaign will not describe MemReel as a prototype, test, research exercise, or product demonstration.

## Core story
Use one specific, relatable scene throughout the experience and creatives:

> Your child is three. At bedtime, you remember how Daadi told you Panchatantra stories until you fell asleep.
>
> Daadi is now an ocean and thousands of miles away.
>
> Childhood will not wait for the next visit.
>
> With MemReel, Daadi can tell those stories in her own voice. Your child can listen, choose, and become part of them.
>
> **One Story, Together. Bring far-away grandparents into story time.**

This becomes the emotional spine, not necessarily one uninterrupted block of text. Copy will follow ASD-STE100-inspired rules: short sentences, familiar words, one idea per line, and direct instructions.

## Product experience
- Replace the current opening with the Daadi and Panchatantra scene.
- Build the opening as a short motion-led sequence: bedtime memory, distance, the cost of waiting, then the MemReel invitation.
- Keep the working three-person flow: parent starts the story, grandparent records it, and child listens and takes part.
- Reword each stage so it feels like a family experience, not a study.
- Remove public-facing words such as **prototype**, **test**, **signal**, **session**, and **research**.
- Rename researcher-facing controls as discreet internal session tools so they do not interrupt the family story.
- Keep audio and activity browser-local. Do not add accounts, sending, or storage.

## Campaign creatives
- Replace both final videos with a new vertical and square version built around the same five beats:
  1. **Your child is three.**
  2. **You remember Daadi’s bedtime stories.**
  3. **Now, an ocean separates them.**
  4. **Childhood will not wait for the next visit.**
  5. **Bring Daadi into story time.**
- End on **One Story, Together · MemReel** with **Start their story** as the invitation.
- Create new matching vertical and square covers.
- Keep the approved original motion-graphics system and code-drawn visual language.
- Use no AI-generated images, stock family imagery, copied reference artwork, or exact mileage claim.
- Keep captions complete for sound-off viewing; revise sound and pacing to support warmth first, then urgency.
- Remove extra labels that compete with the central story.

## Social copy
- Rewrite Instagram and LinkedIn copy around the bedtime scene, the ocean between the family, and the cost of waiting for another visit.
- Present MemReel as the way a far-away grandparent becomes part of story time now.
- Do not use fear-heavy claims about death, permanent loss, or a child forgetting a grandparent.
- Do not mention sharing a prototype or measuring a research signal.

- Inspect the full videos and representative frames for timing, contrast, cropped text, collisions, black bands, and caption readability.
- Listen to the final audio mix, not only measure it.
- Complete the product flow and test playback and downloads on phone, tablet, and desktop.
- Confirm the emotional sequence is clear without sound: remembered bedtime story → physical distance → time pressure → MemReel closes the gap.
